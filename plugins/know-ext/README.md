# What you should know (know-ext)

The summary on top of a brief, as a plugin. Generated by Zeff's `scripts/port-know.mts`.

- The brief (people, open promises, last contacts, related things, held to your sensitivity labels) is Zeff's own: the read `brief.get`, shown by the panel as before.
- The lambda `know.summary` writes the few points; the flow `know.summarize` keeps them by the brief's hash; the flow `know.prepare` makes them before your meetings with the briefing.
- Its AI task is the row "What you should know" of this plugin in Settings → AI; the prompt page (System → Prompts → What you should know) works as before.

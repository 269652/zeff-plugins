# Knowledge (knowledge-ext)

Confluence's pages as a knowledge graph, as a plugin. Generated by Zeff's `scripts/port-knowledge.mts`.

- **Chat** (a page under System, found through the menu's Chat): the knowledge server's state, the conversation (kept in Zeff's AI memo), reading Confluence now, adding something. A question about numbers and dates in your own data is answered from it first (the structured questions); else the knowledge server answers from its pages, citing them.
- **Matching** (the step after the briefing): the lambda `knowledge.match` picks, for each new or changed open todo, the pages and terms that really fit; the flow `knowledge.relate` keeps them on the todo (its Knowledge toggle).
- **Settings**: the knowledge server's address and token — empty means this account's own server.
- Stays Zeff's (the kernel): the connection itself (`service.read`, `service.send`), the export the server reads (Jira, Teams, mail … as pages — `/api/knowledge/export`; a plugin's own pages join it through `contributes.knowledgeDocs`), the panel on a todo, the systems of your repos.
- Its AI task is the row "Knowledge matching" of this plugin in Settings → AI; the prompt page (System → Prompts) works as before.

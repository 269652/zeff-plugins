# Google Drive (google-drive-ext)

The port of Zeff's built-in Google Drive. Generated by Zeff's `scripts/port-google-drive.mts` — change the generator and generate it again.

- **Signing in**: `contributes.oauth` — Zeff's generic sign-in for plugins. The OAuth client (id and secret) is this plugin's settings (⚙️ on
  its card); the kernel serves `/api/ext/oauth/google-drive-ext/drive/connect` and `/callback` (register the callback as the client's redirect
  address — the page shows it), keeps the tokens encrypted and refreshes them. A sign-in the built-in already had is taken over once.
- **Reading Drive**: lambdas (`drive.*`) — they only read: the folders, what is in them, and the *plan* of each sync (what goes up, what comes in).
- **Changing Drive and Zeff**: flows — `google-drive.sync-documents` (Documents ↔ Drive, in the place of the sync step *drivedocs*),
  `google-drive.sync-backups` (Backups → Drive, in the place of *drivebackup*), `google-drive.folder` (Zeff's folder and its Backups / Documents
  folder), `add-folder` / `remove-folder` (the folders of your Drive that come into the documents).
- **What Zeff recorded as its own in Drive** is the built-in's table `google_drive_files`, adopted: nothing goes up twice through the switch.
- **The switch** (*Switch to the plugin* on Google Drive's card): this plugin on, its package installed, the built-in off.

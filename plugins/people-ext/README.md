# People — the AI steps (people-ext)

What the AI does with your people, as a plugin. Generated by Zeff's `scripts/port-people.mts`.

- The people themselves (contacts, claims, links, details, circles, the graph) are Zeff's own — the shared graph every app reads. This plugin reads them through the kernel's flow actions and its flows write with the kernel's.
- Lambdas: `people.extract`, `people.compile`, `people.classify`, `people.names` — the built-in's own instructions, answer shapes and rules, bundled.
- Flows: `people.extract`, `people.compile`, `people.compile-stale`, `people.classify` and `people.normalize` — the Sync step "People matching" in the place of the built-in's.
- Still the built-in's while this plugin is on: the People screens (the list, a person's page, the circles), the widgets, the formulas, the review sources, the tools, the "Compile" button on a person's page.

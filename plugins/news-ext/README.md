# News (news-ext) — slice 1

The sync's heart of Zeff's News, as a plugin. Generated by Zeff's `scripts/port-news.mts`.

- `news.round` fetches the feeds due and works out what is new; `news.rescore` scores everything recent for you with the built-in's own scoring; the flow `news.fetch-round` writes it (SQL over the plugin's stores, which adopt the built-in's tables — nothing copied).
- **Not yet here** (still the built-in's — so there is no *Switch to the plugin*): the agent's filter, clearing away, reading ahead, tags, the overview, discovering feeds, "more like this", your actions on an item, the page and its settings.
- The built-in's tables must be there (the built-in News installed once) for the stores to adopt them.
